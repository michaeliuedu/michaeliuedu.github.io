About Me

My name is Michael Liu, I am an undergraduate studying computer science at Carnegie Mellon University. I am particularly interested in computer systems, competitive programming, and building autonomous systems. Outside of computer science, I fence (competitively for about a decade now), enjoy spending time with friends and family, and embark on projects that challenge me.

Currently, I am a research assistant in the robotics department at Carnegie Mellon University, a web developer for ScottyLabs as part of the tech team for CMUEats.com, and a teaching assistant for CMU's Introduction to Computer Science course. Thanks for checking out my website!
